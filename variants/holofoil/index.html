What Are Holofoil Sprites?

Holofoil Sprites are a special variant of the Sprite companions in Fortnite, distinguished by their shimmering, iridescent shell that catches the light as you move. Unlike the standard versions, this variant is built around a squad-focused perk rather than a solo buff. The verified bonus for Holofoil Sprites is a 5% chance for your whole squad to find rare Sprites from containers, meaning the whole team benefits when one is active. That makes Holofoil Sprites especially appealing for trios and squads who want to grow their Sprite collection faster across a single match, since the perk effectively turns every container you and your teammates open into a better shot at something rare.

How to Get Holofoil Sprites in Fortnite

Holofoil Sprites went live on July 9, 2026, starting with five Sprites: Water, Fire, Ghost, King, and Striker. The v41.20 update on July 16 then added three more — Air, Seven, and Batman — which shipped with their Holofoil finish available day one. (The variant was briefly enabled early on June 25 through Sprite Hunt Rift Anomalies before Epic pulled it the same day, so July 9 marks the real, permanent launch.) Holofoil variants roll from the same container and chest ecosystem that feeds the rest of the Sprite pool, and Epic has been running Holofoil Hours Power Hour windows with boosted spawns on weekends — those are the best sessions to hunt one down. Official drop rates have not been published yet; we will add the exact odds to this page the moment they are listed.

Full List of Holofoil Sprites

This variant spans eleven Sprites across every rarity, all live and collectable right now: Water, Fire, and Air (Rare), Ghost, King, and Striker (Epic), Seven and Peeky Peely (Legendary), and Batman, Grim Reaper, and Zero Point (Mythic). The v41.30 update on July 30, 2026 added the newest three — Peeky Peely's day-one Holofoil plus the long-awaited Grim Reaper and Zero Point finishes. Every published rate sits well under one percent — from 0.25% on the Rares down to 0.01% for Batman, with the three newest still unlisted — so every pull feels like an event.

Are Holofoil Sprites Worth Chasing?

Whether Holofoil Sprites are worth chasing depends on how you play. If you mostly run squads, the container perk can compound across teammates — several Holofoils in one squad means more rare pulls for everyone — making this variant more valuable than a typical solo-only option. Among the live eleven, the Rare trio of Water, Fire, and Air should be the most attainable for completionists, while the Epic trio of Ghost, King, and Striker takes more patience. Seven, Peeky Peely, Batman, Grim Reaper, and Zero Point are the prestige pulls of the current pool. With published odds running from 0.25% on the Rare trio down to 0.01% for Batman — and the three v41.30 additions still unlisted — expect a genuine grind rather than a quick farm.

Tips for Collecting Holofoil Sprites

A few practical tips now that Holofoil Sprites are live. First, play in a squad to make the most of the rare-Sprite container perk that defines the variant. Second, prioritize opening every container you pass, since the value scales with how many you crack open across a match. Third, knock out the Rare Holofoil Water, Fire, and Air early to build momentum before grinding the Epic tier, and play the weekend Holofoil Hours windows when spawns are boosted. Finally, keep your expectations grounded: the odds are unpublished and clearly tight, so patience and consistent matches will matter more than chasing a single lucky drop — and remember a find only counts once you extract it.
