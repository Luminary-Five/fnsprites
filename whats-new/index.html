What's New in Fortnite Sprites

Every new Sprite, variant and event — logged the day it drops. Epic adds new Sprites most Thursdays, so check back weekly. There are currently 25 Sprites across 111 released variants. Track them on the FORTNITE SPRITES TRACKER.

  1. 2026-07-30 v41.30.1 New Sprites

    v41.30.1: Hotpatch Removing IronMouse and Gems — 111 to 109 variants

    • Fortnite Vaulted the IronMouse Sprite and the Gem grim that were released earlier today.
  2. 2026-07-30 v41.30 New Sprites

    v41.30: four new Sprites and the Quack tier — 91 to 111 variants

    • Four new Sprites join the roster: Lootin' Llama (Legendary — opening Ammo Boxes can upgrade your weapon, with a day-one Gem), Peeky Peely (Legendary — marks nearby rare Sprite variants and their carriers but reveals you too, day-one Holofoil), Ironmouse (Mythic collab, ability being confirmed in-game) and John Wick (Mythic — a Reload-mode pickup that reveals nearby enemies after a knock or elimination; once claimed there you can equip it in Battle Royale).
    • The Quack tier is official: unlock it through Sprite Mastery Part 4 for Water, Earth, Fire and Zero Point. Quack bonus: every other Sprite you carry gains an extra 50% of your Quack Sprite’s level progress.
    • Zero Point's Holofoil and Cube plus Grim Reaper's Gem and Holofoil also went live — the collection grows from 91 to 111 released variants across 25 Sprites, and share codes now stamp V5 (old codes keep decoding).
    • Gem drop rates are pre-published for the six pending Gems (Water/Earth 0.37%, Duck/Demon 0.1%, Aura 0.08%, Zero Point 0.00001%) and Cube rates arrived for Earth/Fire/Fishy (0.25%). Punk's Gem icon is back in the files but still unreleased.
    • Events: Peeky Peely Hours run July 30 – August 5 (boosted Peely spawns 4-5 PM and 11 PM-12 AM ET); Web Shooter Hours on August 1 bring boosted Cube spawns and keep the Sprite Hunt Rift Anomaly active.
  3. 2026-07-24 v41.20 Event

    Summon costs cut across the board

    • Base summon costs dropped ~10% in the July 24 hotfix: Epic 3,000 → 2,700, Legendary 5,000 → 4,500, Mythic 7,500 → 6,750 Sprite Dust. Fishy and Air fell from 2,000 to 1,800; the elemental Rares (Water, Earth, Fire) stay at 100.
    • Special-variant summons were cut even deeper: Rare 4,000 → 2,700, Epic 6,000 → 4,000, Legendary 10,000 → 6,750, Mythic 15,000 → 10,000.
    • Leak watch: Gem Punk and Holofoil Zero Point lost their datamined icons on fortnite.gg (placeholder art again), so they leave the pending list. Gem remains confirmed for Water, Earth, Duck, Demon, Aura and Zero Point, plus Quack Zero Point.
    • Roster re-verified against fortnite.gg on July 25: still 21 Sprites / 91 released variants, every published drop rate unchanged, Cube rates still unlisted. Shinier Hours ran two 2× Sprite XP windows on July 25.
  4. 2026-07-23 v41.20 Variant

    Cube variants go live for eight Sprites

    • Cube variants released for Earth, Fire, Dream, Punk, Fishy, Boss, Grim Reaper and Batman — the collection grows from 83 to 91 released variants.
    • Cube is the official name for the tier previously leaked as "Rift" (same art) — the Rift page now lives at /variants/cube.
    • Cube bonus: grants the Overdrive effect while you are in the Storm. Cube drop rates are not published yet.
    • Pollo now spawns from regular chest loot too, and Epic confirmed its ability: eliminations slowly restore Shield for you and nearby teammates.
    • Leak watch: a "Quack" tier appeared in the files (Zero Point has real art). John Wick is still upcoming.
  5. 2026-07-18 v41.20 Event

    Drop rates republished + Sprite event weekend

    • fortnite.gg republished the full drop-rate table on a new basis — every published rate updated (e.g. Duck 9%, Galaxy Zero Point 0.000016%); Rare base Sprites, Grim Reaper specials and Burnt Peanut no longer list a rate.
    • Batman, Air and Seven rates are now published, including all five day-one Holofoil rates (0.25% Rare / 0.06% Epic / 0.05% Legendary / 0.01% Batman).
    • Sprite events announced: Shiny Hours (Jul 18, boosted Special-variant spawn rates), Striker Hour (Jul 19) and Mastery Mondays (Jul 20, faster Sprite upgrades).
    • No new Sprites this week — the roster holds at 21 Sprites / 83 released variants; John Wick is still upcoming.
  6. 2026-07-16 v41.20 New Sprites

    v41.20 — five new Sprites land

    • Air (Rare), Seven (Legendary) and Batman (Mythic) released — each with Normal, Gold, Gummy, Galaxy AND day-one Holofoil variants.
    • Pollo and Vini Jr. joined as single-variant Mythic Sprites (Sprite Chests and Rare Chests).
    • The collection total jumps from 66 to 83 released variants; only John Wick remains upcoming.
    • Fishy summon cost raised from 100 to 2,000 Sprite Dust; drop rates for the new Sprites are not published yet.
  7. 2026-07-12 Site
