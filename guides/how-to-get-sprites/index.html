How to Get Fortnite Sprites: The Complete Collector's Guide

What Are Fortnite Sprites?

Fortnite Sprites are collectible companion creatures and the signature mechanic of Chapter 7 Season 3, "Runners." Each Sprite you equip grants a passive ability that quietly works in your favor during a match, so the right lineup can shift how you play. Right now there are 25 base Sprites spread across 111 released variant rows, and Epic keeps the roster growing by adding more most Thursdays. They come in four rarities: Rare, Epic, Legendary, and Mythic. Because the pool changes so often, many players treat collecting Fortnite Sprites as a season-long hunt rather than a one-and-done task, and the season runs until August 19, 2026.

Every Way to Find Fortnite Sprites

The most reliable source of Fortnite Sprites is the Sprite Chest, and every Sprite Chest is guaranteed to contain one, so they are always worth the detour. Beyond those, Sprites can also appear at lower odds from regular chests, Rare Chests, and supply drops, which means casual looting can still surprise you with a new creature. Defeated players who were carrying a Sprite can drop one too, so finishing a fight is sometimes its own reward. Vaults are another strong option: a Rare Vault gives one Sprite Chest, while an Epic Sprite Vault gives three. If you want to find Fortnite Sprites quickly, prioritizing vaults and dedicated Sprite Chests is the smartest route, but keep opening everything else because the lower-odds sources add up.

You Must Extract Fortnite Sprites to Keep Them

Here is the rule that trips up newcomers: finding a Sprite is not the same as owning it. Once you pick up a Sprite, you must extract it before the match ends, or it is gone for good. You extract Fortnite Sprites at a designated Extraction Site, or you can carry a Portable Extractor and bank your catch on the move when reaching a fixed site is too risky. Treat extraction as the real objective. It is painful to find a stack of Fortnite Sprites, play beautifully, then leave with nothing because you never locked them in. Plan your route around an Extraction Site, or pack a Portable Extractor before hunting Fortnite Sprites in contested zones.

Summoning Fortnite Sprites With Sprite Dust

Once a Sprite is safely extracted and banked, it joins your permanent collection, and you bring it back into play later by summoning it with Sprite Dust. Sprite Dust is the currency that ties the system together, so the more you bank, the more freely you can re-summon your favorite Fortnite Sprites between matches. Certain variants reward you with extra resources, which makes farming smarter rather than just longer. Reported variant bonuses include Gold for 3x elimination Sprite XP, Gummy for +20% Sprite Dust on extraction, Galaxy for +30% ammo on pickup, Gem for reduced fall damage, and Holofoil for a squad chance to find rare Sprites. Those values are reported, so confirm them in-game, but the takeaway is simple: chasing Gummy variants helps you stockpile the Sprite Dust you need to summon Fortnite Sprites whenever you want.

Beginner Tips for Collecting Fortnite Sprites

If you are just starting, keep a few habits in mind. First, land near known vault and chest density so you can chase Fortnite Sprites early while the lobby is still spreading out. Second, never push a fight without an extraction plan, because losing un-banked Fortnite Sprites to a third party stings more than any weapon loss. Third, learn which passive abilities suit your playstyle, since the best lineup of Fortnite Sprites is the one that matches how you fight. Fourth, prioritize Gummy and Gold variants when you spot them, as the bonus Sprite Dust and XP compound over a season. Finally, keep notes on what you still need, because tracking turns a random grind into clear progress.

Frequently asked questions

Do Fortnite Sprites disappear if I don't extract them?

Yes. A Sprite you find only stays yours if you extract it at an Extraction Site or with a Portable Extractor before the match ends. If the match ends while it is un-banked, you lose it, so always plan an extraction route.

What is the fastest way to find Fortnite Sprites?

Open Sprite Chests, since each one is guaranteed to contain a Sprite, and target vaults: a Rare Vault gives one Sprite Chest while an Epic Sprite Vault gives three. Regular chests, Rare Chests, supply drops, and defeated players can also drop them at lower odds.

How do I bring back a Sprite I already collected?

After a Sprite is extracted and banked, you re-summon it later using Sprite Dust. Banking Gummy variants gives +20% Sprite Dust on extraction (reported), so they help you build the currency you need to summon your collection again.
