How to use this Fortnite Sprites list

Every Sprite in the list above is a collectible companion you find in a match, extract at an Extraction Site, and then keep permanently. The rarity column tells you how often a Sprite shows up — Rare Sprites are the most common, Mythic the hardest. The base rate is the drop chance for the Normal version; Gold, Gummy and Galaxy variants are far rarer, which is why a checklist is the only sane way to track a full collection.
