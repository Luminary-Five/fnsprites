What Legendary Sprites Are in Fortnite

Legendary Sprites sit at the top tier of Fortnite's Sprite collection, just below the ultra-rare Mythic class. They are powerful companion drops that follow you through a match and trigger game-changing effects, which is exactly why so many players hunt them and track them on a checklist. Unlike the more common Rare and Epic tiers, Legendary Sprites are designed to swing a fight in your favor, and finding even one in a match feels like a genuine reward.

If you are building out your collection, the goal is simple: spot a Legendary Sprite in the wild, summon it, and add it to your FORTNITE SPRITES TRACKER so you always know which Legendary Sprites you still need.

The Full List of Legendary Sprites

There are six Legendary Sprites in this tier, and each one fills a different role. The Dream Sprite grants a random item each level, with stronger loot waiting at max level, and it appears in chests as a rarer spawn. The Punk Sprite can trigger infinite ammo effects, which makes it a favorite for aggressive players, and it also shows up in chests as a rarer spawn. The Boss Sprite boosts your maximum Health and Shield, giving you a durability edge, and it comes specifically from boss drops rather than ordinary containers. The Seven Sprite (v41.20) reveals enemy foot trails to your whole squad. The two v41.30 newcomers round out the tier: Lootin' Llama gives Ammo Boxes a chance to upgrade your weapon, and Peeky Peely marks nearby rare Sprite variants and their carriers — at the cost of revealing your own position.

Knowing the location of each of these Legendary Sprites is half the battle, because the spawn point dictates how you route across the map.

How to Get Legendary Sprites

Getting Legendary Sprites comes down to landing in the right place and opening the right loot sources. For Dream and Punk, you want to crack open as many chests as possible, since both appear as rarer chest spawns rather than guaranteed pickups. For the Boss Sprite, you need to seek out and defeat the match boss, then claim it from the boss drop. Seven, Lootin' Llama, and Peeky Peely all roll from Sprite Chests and regular chests across the map — and Peely spawns are boosted during the Peeky Peely Hours event windows through August 5.

Because none of these three Legendary Sprites have a fixed summon cost listed, your real currency here is time and chest volume. The more containers you open per match, the better your odds of running into one you are still missing.

How Rare and Strong Legendary Sprites Are

Just how rare are Legendary Sprites? They are noticeably scarcer than the Epic tier and far rarer than common Rare drops. In their standard form, Dream, Punk, Boss, and Seven all carry a 6.98% drop rate. Those numbers are for the base versions, and special themed versions of these Sprites drop at a tiny fraction of those rates.

Strength-wise, the payoff matches the rarity: free loot every level, infinite ammo windows, and a bigger health and shield pool are all effects that meaningfully change how a match plays out. That combination of low availability and high impact is what makes Legendary Sprites so prized among collectors and competitive players alike.

Tips for Collecting All Legendary Sprites

Collecting all of the Legendary Sprites takes patience and a little planning, so here are some tips that actually help. First, prioritize high-traffic chest zones early, because Dream and Punk both rely on chest spawns and volume is your friend. Second, commit to taking on the boss each match if the Boss Sprite is still missing from your set, since it is the only source for that one. Third, play full matches rather than dropping hot and dying fast, as more time alive means more chests and more chances at the Legendary Sprites you still need.

Why Patience Pays Off With Legendary Sprites

Patience really is the theme with Legendary Sprites. Because their drop rates are low, you may go several matches without seeing one, and that is normal. Treat each chest as a small lottery ticket, keep your routes efficient, and let the odds work in your favor over many games.

Over time the three Legendary Sprites will fall into place, and your completed tier will sit proudly alongside your Epic and Rare collections. Keep your tracker open, stay consistent, and the full set of Legendary Sprites is absolutely within reach for any dedicated collector.

Frequently asked questions

How many Legendary Sprites are there in Fortnite?

There are six Legendary Sprites: Dream, Punk, Boss, Seven, Lootin' Llama, and Peeky Peely. Each has a unique ability and its own drop source, so completing the tier means collecting all six.

Which Legendary Sprite is the rarest?

Among the base versions, Dream, Punk, Boss, and Seven all sit at a 6.98% drop rate; the v41.30 newcomers Lootin' Llama and Peeky Peely do not have published rates yet.

Where do I find the Boss Legendary Sprite?

The Boss Sprite comes from boss drops, so you need to locate and defeat the match boss to claim it, unlike Dream and Punk which spawn from chests.
