What the Ironmouse Sprite Is

The Ironmouse Sprite is a Mythic collab companion that appeared with the v41.30 update on July 30, 2026, joining the growing lineup of crossover Mythics alongside Batman, Vini Jr., and John Wick. Unusually, Epic's v41.30 update notes did not describe its ability, and the official wording has not been published yet — we list it as being confirmed in-game and will update this page the moment the effect is verified.

That quiet arrival mirrors how Batman launched during Hot Bat Summer: live in the collection first, details confirmed after. What is certain is its Mythic slot, its single Normal finish, and that it counts toward the 111-variant collection right now.

How to Get the Ironmouse Sprite

The Ironmouse Sprite rolls from Sprite Chests and chests across the map, with no published drop rate. Mythic pulls are rare by design, so favor Sprite Chests — they guarantee an Epic-or-better result — and extract every find at an Extraction Site, with a Portable Extractor, or by winning the match. An unextracted Ironmouse is an Ironmouse you never owned.

Is the Ironmouse Sprite Worth Chasing?

As a single-variant Mythic, it is one grab for a full family — the same easy math as Pollo and Vini Jr. Collab Sprites also tend to be tied to event windows, so the safer play is to secure it early rather than bet on it staying in the pool all season.
