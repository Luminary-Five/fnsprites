What the Earth Sprite Is and Its Rarity

The Earth Sprite is a Rare-tier companion that first arrived in patch v41.00, sitting alongside the other elemental Rare Sprites in the current loot pool. As a Rare collectible, it is far more common than Epic, Legendary, or Mythic options, which makes the Earth Sprite a sensible early target for newer collectors who want a reliable pickup without grinding for ultra-low odds. Thematically it ties into the natural world, and its home turf reflects that.

What sets this little buddy apart is its loot-focused identity. Rather than a flashy combat trick, the Earth Sprite leans into improving the quality of what you find on the ground, which fits cleanly with a looting-heavy playstyle.

How to Find and Extract the Earth Sprite

You will find the Earth Sprite in forests and wooded areas, so plan your drops around tree-heavy zones rather than open fields or city blocks. Sweeping the green pockets of the map gives you the best shot at spotting one in the wild. If random spawns are not cooperating, you can summon the Earth Sprite for a cost of 100, letting you guarantee an attempt instead of relying purely on luck.

Once you have located it, the goal is extraction. Securing and extracting the Earth Sprite is what banks it into your collection and rewards Sprite Dust, so survive the encounter, complete the extraction, and the catch is yours to keep.

The Earth Sprite Ability and Whether It Is Worth Using

The Earth Sprite ability gives a chance for extra rare items from chests. In practice that means every chest you crack carries a bonus roll at better gear, quietly upgrading your loadout over the course of a match. Because it is passive and stacks naturally with normal looting, you do not have to change how you play to benefit.

Is it worth using? If you open a lot of chests and value a stronger kit by mid-game, the Earth Sprite earns its slot. It will not win a fight on its own the way an aggressive Epic might, but for loot-driven and rotation-heavy players the consistent gear upside is genuinely useful. Casual lobbies and chest-rush routes get the most out of it.

Every Variant of the Earth Sprite With Drop Rates and Bonuses

The Earth Sprite comes in several themed finishes, each with its own rarity-style odds and perks. The Normal version is the baseline and carries the standard ability with no extra bonus; its drop rate is no longer published. Gold is the prestige finish at 0.75%, adding 3x bonus Sprite XP from eliminations to help you level faster.

The collector-favorite themes get rarer from there. Gummy sits at 0.62% and grants +20% Sprite Dust on extraction, while Galaxy is 0.5% and gives +30% more ammo on pickup. The Cube Earth Sprite has been live since July 23, 2026 — its rate was published at 0.25% on July 30 — and grants Overdrive while you are in the Storm. The Quack Earth Sprite followed on July 30 as a Sprite Mastery Part 4 unlock that shares +50% of its level progress with your other carried Sprites. A Gem variant is still pending with a pre-published 0.37% rate and a reduced fall damage bonus, so a complete set here means Normal, Gold, Gummy, Galaxy, Cube, Quack, and eventually Gem.

Tips and Strategy for Chasing the Earth Sprite

Set up your route before the match. Landing in or near forests and wooded areas puts you on the Earth Sprite hunt immediately, and keeping 100 in reserve to summon means you are never fully at the mercy of spawns. Prioritize the basic catch first, then circle back for the shinier themes.

For the rare finishes, repetition is everything: more forest visits and more summons mean more chances at Gold, Gummy, and Galaxy. Pair the chase with chest-heavy rotations so the Earth Sprite ability is paying you out while you grind.
