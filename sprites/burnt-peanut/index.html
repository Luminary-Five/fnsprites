What Is the Burnt Peanut Sprite and How Rare Is It?

The Burnt Peanut Sprite is one of the most coveted collectibles in the current Fortnite Sprite lineup, and it carries a Mythic rarity rating. That places it at the very top tier alongside the rarest companions in the set, which is exactly why so many players keep this one on their wishlist. Introduced in the v41.00 patch, the Sprite has become a genuine status symbol for collectors who want to show off a complete shelf.

Mythic status means the Burnt Peanut Sprite does not show up casually during a normal match. Its standard form's drop rate is no longer published, but you can still expect long stretches between sightings. If you are working through your collection, this is the kind of entry that often gets saved for last simply because it demands patience and a bit of luck.

How to Find and Extract the Burnt Peanut Sprite

Finding the Burnt Peanut Sprite comes down to one specific source: Relic Chests. Unlike Sprites that spawn from ordinary loot or scattered containers, this companion is tied to these special chests, so your route planning should prioritize locations where Relic Chests appear. Landing with a plan and heading straight for those chests gives you far more attempts per session than wandering the map hoping for a lucky pull.

Once a Relic Chest reveals the Burnt Peanut Sprite, extraction works like any other Sprite capture, but the stakes feel higher because of the rarity. Survive the encounter, secure the extraction, and it is added to your account. There is no summon cost listed, so you do not need to bank a resource pool ahead of time. The real cost is the time spent farming Relic Chests until the roll finally lands in your favor.

The Burnt Peanut Sprite Ability and Is It Worth Using?

The Burnt Peanut Sprite ability can drop extra or mythic loot on eliminations, which makes it one of the more aggressive, reward-driven companions you can equip. Every knock you secure becomes a potential jackpot, turning fights into loot opportunities rather than just survival checkpoints. For players who push engagements and rack up eliminations, this ability rewards exactly the playstyle they already enjoy.

Is it worth using? If you play aggressively, absolutely. The Burnt Peanut Sprite shines in lobbies where you are taking fights early and often, because the extra or mythic loot it can produce snowballs your kit. Passive or stealth-focused players may see the ability trigger less often, but even then it remains a prestige pick that signals you have earned one of the rarest Sprites available.

Every Variant of the Burnt Peanut Sprite With Drop Rates

Collectors often ask about Gold, Gummy, and Galaxy versions, but here is the honest answer: the Burnt Peanut Sprite currently has a single released variant, the Normal theme. There is no Gold, Gummy, Galaxy, Gem, Holofoil, or Rift form listed at this time, which actually simplifies your goal. You are chasing exactly one form rather than a long ladder of recolors.

The Normal Burnt Peanut Sprite provides the standard Sprite ability with no extra cosmetic bonus layered on top; its drop rate is no longer published. Because there is only the one theme, completing this entry means a single successful pull rather than grinding multiple rarities. That single-variant structure makes the Burnt Peanut Sprite unusual among Mythic entries, and it is worth logging the moment you secure it so your records stay accurate.

Tips and Strategy for Chasing the Burnt Peanut Sprite

Since it only drops from Relic Chests, maximizing the number of Relic Chests you open per match matters far more than any single perfect game.
