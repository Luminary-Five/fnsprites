Fortnite Sprite Drop Rates

The full Fortnite Sprite drop-rate chart for every released variant. Normal Sprites are common; Gold, Gummy and especially Galaxy variants get vanishingly rare — the rarest, Galaxy Zero Point, sits at 0.0000160%. Track what you've pulled on the FORTNITE SPRITES TRACKER.

How Sprite drop rates work

Sprites drop from Sprite Chests (guaranteed one each) and, at lower odds, from regular chests, Rare Chests, supply drops and defeated players. The percentages below are the chance of a specific Sprite variant appearing. Because Galaxy and other premium variants are hundreds of times rarer than the Normal version, completion hunters lean on the drop-rate chart to decide what to chase — and the FORTNITE SPRITES TRACKER to record it.
